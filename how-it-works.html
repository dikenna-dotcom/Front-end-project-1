The workflow

The proposed UAR workflow moves from requirements to a documented decision.

This page describes a concept for review. It does not run an application or produce a live compatibility verdict.

  1. 1

    Describe the application

    Record its purpose, known dependencies and the requirements that may affect where it can operate.

  2. 2

    Compare with an environment

    Consider known technical requirements against a defined target, and record what is missing or uncertain.

  3. 3

    Review policy and risk

    Ask whether security, privacy and operational rules are met. Technical compatibility does not answer these questions by itself.

  4. 4

    Consider a suitable strategy

    A responsible operator can compare options against the requirements and conditions identified in the review.

  5. 5

    Record the rationale

    Keep the evidence, assumptions, limitations and open questions together so the decision can be revisited.

Important decision rule

Compatibility is not the same as safety.

An app can be compatible with a platform while still posing security or privacy concerns. UAR keeps those questions separate so visitors can see why a system may be technically aligned but still not acceptable under policy.

Separate the questions

An application may meet technical requirements but still raise privacy, security or policy concerns. A compatibility label is not a safety certification.
