Keyboard checks at actual zoom
- Tab focused the visible skip link.
- Enter activated it and navigated to #main-content.
- Subsequent Tab navigation focused the first FAQ summary.
- Enter collapsed the disclosure; a second Enter reopened it.
- On the Features planner, the form fit inside the 253px content width: form bounds were x=10px to x=243.7px, and text inputs/buttons were within those bounds. A focused planner screenshot is linked in the evidence index.

Conclusion
The measured 400% run passed the no-horizontal-overflow and tested keyboard paths at a viewport narrower than the nominal 320px target. Normal vertical scrolling was required and reached the page footers. This result is local-browser evidence only; it is not a screen-reader review, physical-device test, hosted-site test or full-page visual audit.
