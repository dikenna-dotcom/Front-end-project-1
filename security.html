Security

Technical fit does not settle security or privacy questions.

The UAR concept keeps those decisions visible. This static site offers considerations, not a security assessment or certification.

Policy alignment

Identify the rules that apply to the environment and determine who is responsible for reviewing them.

Privacy impact

Ask what information an application can access, how it handles data and what users need to know.

Risk transparency

Any outcome should explain its evidence, limitations and remaining questions instead of implying certainty.

Key principle

Compatibility is not safety.

An application may meet technical requirements while still conflicting with privacy, security or organisational rules. The proposed UAR workflow treats these as separate review questions; this website does not perform those checks.

Examples of policy questions

  • What data would the app handle?
  • What access or permissions are required?
  • Does the environment impose restrictions?
  • Who can review and approve any proposed mitigations?

Honest limits

What this site does not do

Not a live scanner

This static website does not inspect a visitor’s computer or analyse uploads.

Not a guarantee

It describes a decision model; no live result, safety guarantee or universal support is provided here.
